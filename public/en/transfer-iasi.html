FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Complete answers before booking your Transfer Iasi - Chisinau transfer.

How much does the Transfer Iasi - Chisinau transfer cost?+
We offer 3 packages: Standard Private €180, Business Comfort €220 (most popular) and VIP Executive €280. All prices are per vehicle (4+1 seats), all-inclusive: WiFi, water, Sculeni-Albita border assistance. No hidden costs. Pay on arrival.
How much is the transfer from Chișinău to Iași?+
Same price in both directions: Standard €180, Business €220, VIP €280 per car, from central Chișinău to Iași city or Iași Airport (IAS). Split between 4 passengers, Standard is €45 per person. The drive takes ~3–3.5 hours, including 30–60 minutes at the Sculeni–Albița border.
How long is the drive from Iasi to Chisinau?+
Distance is ~130 km. Actual driving: 2–2.5 hours. At Sculeni-Albita border: 30–60 minutes. Total: ~3–3.5 hours. Our drivers cross the border daily and know all procedures to minimize waiting time.
How many passengers can travel?+
Our vehicles seat 4+1 seats (4 passengers + driver). Price is per vehicle, not per personwhether you travel alone or with 4 people, the cost stays the same. Luggage included with no limits.
How does border assistance work?+
Border crossing point is Sculeni-Albita. Valid passport required. Process takes 30–60 minutes on average. Our drivers assist with formalities, explain the process and help with documents if needed. Border assistance is included in all packages.
What vehicles do you use for transfers?+
Premium 4+1 seat vehicles: Audi A4 / Toyota Auris (Standard), Audi A6 2022 / Lexus NX300h (Business), Mercedes E-Class / BMW 7 Series (VIP). All with climate control, 4G WiFi, chargers, clean and inspected daily.
Can I book a round-trip transfer?+
Yes. We offer round-trip with 4–8 hours wait. Prices: Standard €335, Business €390, VIP €440. Round-trip benefits from an automatic -10% discount compared to two separate transfers.
How do I pay?+
No prepayment for individual bookings. Pay the driver at the end of the trip, cash (EUR or MDL) or bank card. For companies with recurring bookings, we offer monthly invoicing.
Do drivers speak English?+
All drivers speak Romanian and Russian. English is available on requestplease mention when booking. With VIP Executive, multilingual drivers (EN/RO/RU) are included by default.
Do I need a visa to cross the Moldova-Romania border?+
Citizens of the EU, UK, US, Canada, Japan and Australia can enter Moldova visa-free for up to 90 days. Other nationalities should check requirements in advance. A valid passport is required for everyone. PlusRent drivers assist with border procedures and explain requirements.
Do you accept card payments?+
Yes, we accept all major bank cards (Visa, Mastercard, Maestro). Payment occurs upon arrival at destination. We also accept cash in EUR or MDLno commissions or surcharges for any payment method.
Can you take me to Castel Mimi / Cricova / Mileștii Mici from Iași?+
Yes. We provide private transfer to all major Moldovan wineries from Iași Airport or any location in Iași. Castel Mimi (Bulboaca): €195 Standard, €230 Business, €290 VIP. Cricova Cellars: €175. Mileștii Mici: €185. Prices are per vehicle (4+1 seats), not per person. Important: We provide transportation only, winery tours, tastings, and entry fees are booked separately, directly with the wineries (advance reservation recommended).
