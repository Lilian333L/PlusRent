Do I need a visa to cross the Moldova-Romania border?
Everyone needs a valid passport. Citizens of Moldova with a biometric passport have travelled to Romania and the rest of the EU without a visa since April 2014, for up to 90 days in any 180. Citizens of the EU, UK, US, Canada, Japan and Australia enter Moldova without a visa for up to 90 days in any 180. Romania has been in the Schengen area since 1 January 2025, so non-EU travellers entering Romania are registered in the EU Entry/Exit System (fingerprints and a photo) the first time. Citizens of Russia and most CIS countries need a visa to enter Romania (a valid Schengen visa is accepted), so check your own requirements before the trip. PlusRent drivers assist with the border procedures.
