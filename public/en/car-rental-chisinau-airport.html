Car rental at Chisinau Airport (KIV)

You pick the car up in the terminal, at the arrivals exit. Delivery costs €15 and is free from 7 days of rental, with nothing to pay in advance.

In short: PlusRent delivers the rental car straight to Chisinau International Airport (KIV). Delivery and return cost €15 and are free on rentals of 7 days or more, during working hours (08:00 to 18:00). Rates start at €15 a day and include RCA insurance, 200 km a day, air conditioning, GPS and 24/7 roadside assistance. No advance payment is taken, and drivers aged 35 and over leave no security deposit. Bookings round the clock: +373 60 000 500.

How you collect the car at Chisinau Airport

  1. Book online or by phone. Pick the model from the catalogue and give your landing date and time. Booking takes under two minutes and asks for no payment up front.
  2. Send us the flight number. We track the flight, so a delayed plane does not change your booking and costs nothing extra.
  3. We meet you at the arrivals exit. Our colleague is waiting with the car ready, technically checked and cleaned.
  4. Sign and drive off. The contract is signed on the spot in 5 to 10 minutes. You pay at the end, cash in lei or euro, or by card.

What airport car rental costs

The car rate is the same as in town. Delivery to the terminal costs €15 and is not charged on rentals of 7 days or more during working hours. The Economy category starts at €30 a day for one or two days and comes down to €15 a day on rentals over 46 days. Dacia Duster crossover: €25 to €45 a day. Audi A4 business: €40 to €60 a day. Premium, meaning Audi A6, Lexus NX 300h and BMW X5: €50 to €90 a day.

The full rate card by category and rental length is in the price table in the catalogue, next to live availability for every model.

What the price includes and what is charged separately

Included in the rate: compulsory RCA insurance, 200 km a day, air conditioning, GPS navigation, 24-hour roadside assistance. Delivery to Chisinau Airport is included on rentals of 7 days or more during working hours.

Charged separately: airport delivery or collection, €15, free only from 7 days of rental during working hours, and delivery to Iasi Airport (IAS) in Romania, which costs €175. Kilometres above the daily allowance are counted when the car comes back. All of these are quoted before the booking is confirmed, not at the end.

What documents you need

A valid driving licence and an ID card or passport. The minimum age is 18. From 35 years old no security deposit is held; under 35 a refundable deposit is taken and returned in full when the car comes back in the same condition. A bank card is not required: you can pay cash, in lei or in euro.

Pick up at the airport, drop off in town

You can take the car at the airport and leave it in Chisinau, or the other way round, at no extra charge. If you are driving on to Romania, we can collect the car at Iasi Airport for €175. For return trips between Chisinau and Iasi there is also a driven option, described on the Chisinau to Iasi transfer page.

Car rental or an airport transfer?

These are two different services and they are priced differently. Car rental means you take the car and drive yourself, from €15 a day. A transfer means our driver takes you, at a fixed price per trip: 45€ is the price for the airport to Chisinau city centre route in a Standard car, and other districts and towns have their own prices in the table on the transfer page. Choose the rental if you need a car for several days, and the transfer if you only need the ride from the plane to your address.

Frequently asked questions about airport car rental

Can I pick the car up inside Chisinau Airport?

Yes. We bring the car to Chisinau International Airport (KIV) and meet you at the arrivals exit. Delivery costs €15 and is free on rentals of 7 days or more, during working hours (08:00 to 18:00). All we need is your flight number so we can track the landing.

How much does airport delivery cost?

Delivery costs €15 whatever the car category, and is free on rentals of 7 days or more handed over during working hours (08:00 to 18:00). Delivery to Iasi Airport (IAS) in Romania costs €175.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

Nothing changes in your booking and you pay nothing extra. We track the flight number you give when booking and move the meeting time to the actual landing.

Do I need a bank card or a deposit?

A bank card is not required: you can pay cash, in lei or euro, when you collect the car. No security deposit is held from age 35 up. Under 35 a refundable deposit is taken and returned in full at drop-off.

Can I return the car somewhere other than the airport?

Yes. Collecting at the airport and returning in Chisinau, or the other way round, costs nothing extra. Returning at Iasi Airport is charged at €175.

What is the minimum age to rent?

Eighteen, with a valid driving licence. From 35 no security deposit is held. Between 18 and 34 a refundable deposit is taken.

Is it cheaper to rent a car or to take a transfer?

It depends how many journeys you make. A transfer from the airport to Chisinau city centre costs 45€ per trip in a Standard car, roughly €40 for a single ride. A day of Economy rental starts at €30 and leaves the car with you the whole time. If you only need the ride from the plane to your hotel, the transfer is simpler; if you are staying several days, the rental costs less per day.

What is included in the price?

Compulsory RCA insurance, 200 km a day, air conditioning, GPS navigation, 24-hour roadside assistance and airport delivery during working hours. No advance payment is taken: you pay when you collect the car.
