How do you get from Chisinau Airport to the city centre?

Chisinau International Airport (KIV) is 13-15 km from the city centre, about 25 minutes by car. There are three options: trolleybus no. 30, the cheapest; an app taxi, priced by the app according to time and demand; or a PlusRent private transfer at a fixed €45 per car, booked in advance.

How much is a transfer from Chisinau Airport to the city centre?
A transfer from Chisinau Airport to the city centre or Botanica costs €45 in Standard (800 MDL), €65 in Business (1,200 MDL) and €85 in VIP (1,550 MDL). Buiucani, Rîșcani and Ciocana are €55 in Standard, with Business and VIP at the same price. The price is for the whole car with 4+1 seats and includes waiting time, Wi-Fi, water, a charging cable and help with luggage. The centre is 13-15 km away, about 25-30 minutes.
How do I get from Chisinau Airport to the city centre?
The airport is 13-15 km from the centre, about 25-30 minutes by car. You have three options: trolleybus no. 30, the cheapest, with your luggage on your lap and stops along the way; a taxi booked through an app, priced by the app according to time and demand; or a private transfer booked in advance at a fixed price: with PlusRent €45 per car to the centre, with a driver who tracks your flight and waits 30 minutes for free.
How much is a taxi from Chisinau Airport to the city?
For one person with little luggage, an app taxi is usually cheaper. The PlusRent transfer, though, has a fixed price per car that you know in advance: €45 to the centre, about €11 each when there are four of you. Waiting for a delayed flight, help with luggage and, in Business and VIP, a name sign in the terminal are all included.
What is included in the transfer price?
All transfers include: premium car (Audi A4 on Standard, Audi A6 / Lexus NX300h on Business, Mercedes E-Class / BMW 5 on VIP), professional multilingual driver (EN/RO/RU), 4G WiFi, bottled water, USB chargers, luggage assistance, 30 min free wait (60 min for VIP). No hidden fees.
How far in advance should I book?
Recommended at least 4–6 hours in advance to secure your preferred vehicle. For urgent bookings (under 2 hours), availability is limited, please call directly: +373 60 000 500. Service available non-stop, 24/7, including holidays.
Do you offer transfers from other Moldovan cities?
Yes, all over Moldova, in both directions. Standard prices from the airport: the suburbs (Codru, Sîngera, Ialoveni) from €60, Stăuceni, Dumbrava and Cricova €65, Strășeni €70, Hîncești €80, Orhei €80, Comrat €95, Bălți €110, Soroca €121, Cahul €132. For other places, call or message us and you get the price in a few minutes.
How many passengers can travel?
Our vehicles have 4+1 seats. The price is per vehicle, not per person, whether you travel alone or with 4 people, the cost is the same. Luggage is unlimited (up to 6 standard suitcases). No extra charges.
How does Meet & Greet work at the airport?
On Business and VIP, the driver greets you in the terminal holding a sign with your name, helps with your luggage and walks you directly to the vehicle. On Standard, the driver waits in the airport parking lot and texts you the exact location upon landing.
How do I pay for the transfer?
No advance payment for individual bookings. You pay the driver at the end of the ride, cash (MDL or EUR) or bank card. For corporations and recurring bookings, we offer monthly invoicing options. The fixed price is guaranteed.
What happens if my flight is delayed?
We monitor flights in real time. If your flight is delayed, the driver waits at no extra charge, 30 min included on Standard and Business, 60 min on VIP. After that: 50 MDL per additional 15 minutes.
What exactly does the 45€ price cover?
€45 is the Standard price for the route from Chisinau International Airport (KIV) to the city centre or Botanica, for the whole vehicle, not per passenger. Buiucani, Rîșcani and Ciocana are €55, the suburbs from €60, and towns have their own prices in the table. On the same route to the centre, Business is €65 and VIP €85.
Why does the price differ between Chișinău districts?
Because it follows the real road from the airport and the time spent in traffic. The centre and Botanica are closest with the easiest way in, €45. Buiucani, Rîșcani and Ciocana mean crossing the city, with rush-hour traffic, €55. Every route has a fixed price agreed before you leave, so the amount does not change in traffic.
Is the price per car or per person?
Per car. A Standard transfer carries up to 4 passengers with luggage for the same amount. For four people, 45€ to the centre works out at 200 MDL each. For larger groups or bulky luggage a roomier car is used and the price is quoted when you book.
What if I need the car all day, not just the ride from the airport?
That is not a transfer any more but a personal driver, from 120€ a day with your own car or from 175€ a day with a PlusRent car, minimum 8 hours. If you would rather drive yourself, the option is car rental delivered to the airport, from €15 a day.
Can you take me to the airport too, not only from it?
Yes, both ways at the same price. We pick you up from any address, hotel or town in Moldova. Plan to be at the airport at least 2 hours before your flight; the dispatcher sets the pickup time from your address and the traffic.
Do you have child seats?
Yes. A child seat is included in the price in every class, at no extra charge. Tell us the child's age when you book, and the dispatcher confirms that the right seat is available for your flight date and time.
Is the transfer more expensive at night or at weekends?
No. The price is the same day and night, at weekends and on holidays: from €45 in Standard, €65 in Business and €85 in VIP to the centre. The dispatcher works 24/7, so a 3 a.m. flight is met at the same fixed price.
