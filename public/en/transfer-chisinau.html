FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Quick answers before you book.

How much does a Chisinau Airport, Chisinau transfer cost?+
Chisinau Airport, Chisinau City Center transfer: Standard €45 / 800 MDL, Business Comfort €55 / 1,000 MDL, VIP Executive €75 / 1,400 MDL. All prices are all-inclusive, WiFi, water, luggage assistance, wait time included. Distance 13–15 km, duration 25–30 minutes.
What is included in the transfer price?+
All transfers include: premium car (Toyota Auris / Audi A4 on Standard, Audi A6 / Lexus NX300h on Business, Mercedes E-Class / BMW 5 / Audi A8 on VIP), professional multilingual driver (EN/RO/RU), 4G WiFi, bottled water, USB chargers, luggage assistance, 30 min free wait (60 min for VIP). No hidden fees.
How far in advance should I book?+
Recommended at least 4–6 hours in advance to secure your preferred vehicle. For urgent bookings (under 2 hours), availability is limited, please call directly: +373 60 000 500. Service available non-stop, 24/7, including holidays.
Do you offer transfers from other Moldovan cities?+
Yes, we cover all of Moldova. Transfer to Chisinau Airport from: Balti from €110 / 2,000 MDL, Cahul from €132 / 2,400 MDL, Comrat from €88 / 1,600 MDL, Soroca from €121 / 2,200 MDL, Orhei from €45 / 800 MDL. For special routes, please call.
How many passengers can travel?+
Our vehicles have 4+1 seats. The price is per vehicle, not per person, whether you travel alone or with 4 people, the cost is the same. Luggage is unlimited (up to 6 standard suitcases). No extra charges.
How does Meet & Greet work at the airport?+
On Business and VIP, the driver greets you in the terminal holding a sign with your name, helps with your luggage and walks you directly to the vehicle. On Standard, the driver waits in the airport parking lot and texts you the exact location upon landing.
How do I pay for the transfer?+
No advance payment for individual bookings. You pay the driver at the end of the ride, cash (MDL or EUR) or bank card. For corporations and recurring bookings, we offer monthly invoicing options. The fixed price is guaranteed.
What happens if my flight is delayed?+
We monitor flights in real time. If your flight is delayed, the driver waits at no extra charge, 30 min included on Standard and Business, 60 min on VIP. After that: 50 MDL per additional 15 minutes.
What exactly does the 45€ price cover?+
45€ is the price for the Chisinau International Airport (KIV) to Chisinau city centre route, in a Standard car, for the whole vehicle rather than per passenger. It is not a flat fare to any address in the city: Botanica and Buiucani are 47€, Rascani and Ciocana 49€, and the towns around Chisinau have their own prices in the table above. A Business Comfort car on the same route to the centre is 55€, and VIP Executive 75€.
Why does Botanica or Rascani cost more than the centre?+
Because the price follows the real distance from the airport. The centre is about 13 to 15 km away, Botanica and Buiucani slightly further, and Rascani and Ciocana are the most distant of the districts. Every route has a fixed price quoted before departure, so the amount does not change in traffic and does not depend on how long the drive takes.
Is the price per car or per person?+
Per car. A Standard transfer carries up to 4 passengers with luggage for the same amount. For four people, 45€ to the centre works out at 200 MDL each. For larger groups or bulky luggage a roomier car is used and the price is quoted when you book.
What if I need the car all day, not just the ride from the airport?+
That is not a transfer any more but a personal driver, from 120€ a day with your own car or from 175€ a day with a PlusRent car, minimum 8 hours. If you would rather drive yourself, the option is car rental delivered to the airport, from €15 a day.

What is the PlusRent airport transfer in Chisinau?

The PlusRent airport transfer is a private fixed-price ride between Chisinau International Airport (KIV) and any address, operating 24/7 since 2022. The airport is 13 to 15 km from the city centre, about 25 to 30 minutes by car. Prices for 2026 are per vehicle with 4+1 seats: Standard €45 (800 MDL), Business €55, VIP €75 to the centre. For example, four passengers on Standard pay about 200 MDL each.

The transfer works in three steps. First, book with your flight number by phone or WhatsApp at +373 60 000 500. Second, PlusRent tracks the flight, so a delay changes nothing. Finally, the driver meets you; free waiting is 30 minutes on Standard and Business and 60 minutes on VIP.

Other districts and towns have their own fixed prices, from Botanica €47 to Cahul €132, and a round trip saves 10%. Payment is made at the end of the ride, in cash or by card, with no prepayment. Our booking data shows 1754 completed orders.
