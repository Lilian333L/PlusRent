Chișinău to Tiraspol with a private driver: a day trip to Transnistria

Yes, we cross into Transnistria with a personal driver, and we go anywhere in the region: Tiraspol, Bender and its fortress, Rîbnița, Dubăsari, Grigoriopol, the Kvint brandy distillery. We leave Chisinau and pass the checkpoint at Bender or Ribnita, whichever suits the route. Tiraspol is about 70 km away, roughly an hour and a quarter of driving, not counting the time at the checkpoint.

At the checkpoint you fill in a free entry slip, for which you need a passport or an ID card. It usually takes between ten minutes and half an hour depending on the queue. Our driver has made the trip hundreds of times and knows the procedure, so none of it falls to you. There and back in the same day fits inside the 8 hour package.

On our car175€ + 25€ for the crossing = 200€ / 8 hours

The 25€ covers the longer drive and the time at the checkpoint. It is not a fee we pass on.

Across the border to Romania: Iași and further

We drive to Iași and further into Romania with a personal driver, there and back in one day or over several days. You need a valid passport; the driver and the car are already insured for international travel, with no extra insurance to buy.

The crossing adds 25€, as it does for Transnistria. Every package includes 200 km a day, and each kilometre above that costs 5 MDL: Chisinau to Iasi and back is about 260 km, so about 60 km extra, 300 MDL. For a one-way ride to Iași airport, the Iași transfer is the simpler choice.

Cricova winery tour with a private driver: Cricova, Milestii Mici, Castel Mimi

All three wineries fit into one day on the 8 hour package if we start in the morning. Cricova is 15 km from the centre of Chisinau, Milestii Mici 20 km to the south, and Castel Mimi 45 km away at Bulboaca. All three have underground galleries, and at Milestii Mici you drive through the tunnels, which means we take the car inside.

Tastings are booked separately, directly with the winery, and are not included in the driver price. What we handle is the driving, the waiting between visits and getting you home afterwards, which matters most on a day of tastings. If you want one winery and back, the 8 hour package is more than enough.
