Where we take you with a personal driver

The same 8, 10 or 12 hour packages, on the routes we drive most. The price below is the package; anything extra is added only if you use it.

Crossing into Transnistria: Tiraspol and Bender

Yes, we cross into Transnistria with a personal driver. We leave Chisinau, pass the checkpoint at Bender or Ribnita and go where you need: Tiraspol, Bender fortress, the Kvint brandy distillery. Tiraspol is about 70 km away, roughly an hour and a quarter of driving, not counting the time at the checkpoint.

At the checkpoint you fill in a free entry slip, for which you need a passport or an ID card. It usually takes between ten minutes and half an hour depending on the queue. Our driver has made the trip hundreds of times and knows the procedure, so none of it falls to you. There and back in the same day fits inside the 8 hour package.

A day at the wineries: Cricova, Milestii Mici, Castel Mimi

All three wineries fit into one day on the 8 hour package if we start in the morning. Cricova is 15 km from the centre of Chisinau, Milestii Mici 20 km to the south, and Castel Mimi 45 km away at Bulboaca. All three have underground galleries, and at Milestii Mici you drive through the tunnels, which means we take the car inside.

Tastings are booked separately, directly with the winery, and are not included in the driver price. What we handle is the driving, the waiting between visits and getting you home afterwards, which matters most on a day of tastings. If you want one winery and back, the 8 hour package is more than enough.
