Choosing the right car

We help you pick the right car from our fleet. For general car rental questions, see the home page.

What car categories are in the PlusRent fleet?

The fleet is organised into three categories: Economy (from €15/day), Toyota Auris, Volkswagen Golf Plus, Dacia Duster; Standard (from €40/day), Audi A4; Premium (from €50/day), Audi A6, Lexus NX 300h, BMW X5. All categories include third-party liability (RCA) insurance, 200 km/day mileage and 24/7 support.

What car brands are available for rent?

BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, Porsche, Volkswagen, Skoda, Toyota, Hyundai, Honda. Sedans, crossovers, SUVs and hatchbacks with automatic or manual transmission, in petrol, diesel and hybrid configurations.

How do I choose the right car from the fleet?

Use the catalog filters: brand, body type, transmission, fuel, seats and doors, price. You can pick an entire category (Economy, Standard, Premium) or a specific model. The selection takes under a minute.

What's the difference between Premium and Standard?

Premium (Audi A6, Lexus NX 300h, BMW X5), newer model years, extended option package, leather interior, more powerful engines. Standard (Audi A4), reliable family cars with a basic option package. Pricing: Premium from €50/day, Standard from €40/day.

Can I book a specific car model?

Yes. On the catalog page, click the car you want, then "Rent now". If your chosen model is temporarily unavailable, the manager will offer the closest alternative in the same category at the same price.

Are there electric or hybrid cars in the fleet?

Yes, the PlusRent fleet includes hybrid and plug-in hybrid (PlugIn-Hybrid) models. Use the "Fuel" filter in the catalog to see all electrified cars currently available.

What body types are available?

The fleet includes: sedans (Audi A4/A6, BMW 3/5 Series, Mercedes E-Class), crossovers and SUVs (BMW X1/X3/X5, Audi Q5/Q7, Mercedes GLE), hatchbacks (VW Polo, Skoda Fabia, Toyota Yaris) and minivans for larger groups (up to 7-9 seats).

How much does car rental in Chisinau cost?

The daily rate starts at €15 a day for the Economy category on rentals over 46 days, and at €30 a day for one or two days. Dacia Duster crossover: €45 a day short term, €25 long term. Audi A4 business class: between €60 and €40 a day. Premium (Audi A6, Lexus NX 300h, BMW X5): from €90 a day short term and from €50 a day over 46 days. Every rate includes RCA insurance, 200 km a day, air conditioning, GPS navigation and round-the-clock roadside assistance.

Does the price drop if I rent the car for more days?

Yes. The daily rate steps down five times: 1 to 2 days, 3 to 7 days, 8 to 20 days, 21 to 45 days, and 46 days or more. A Toyota Auris, for example, goes from €35 a day on a two-day rental to €15 a day on a rental over 46 days, less than half. The reduction is applied automatically when the booking is priced; you do not have to ask for it.

What is included in the rental price?

Compulsory RCA insurance, a 200 km per day allowance, air conditioning, GPS navigation and 24-hour roadside assistance. The car is handed over technically checked and cleaned. Delivery to Chisinau International Airport (KIV) costs €15 and is free from 7 days of rental, at any hour. Extra kilometres, delivery between 18:00 and 08:00 and delivery to Iasi Airport (€175) are charged separately and quoted before you book.

Do I have to pay a deposit or anything in advance?

No advance payment: you pay when you collect the car, in cash (lei or euro) or by bank card. The security deposit is zero for drivers aged 35 and over on rentals of 7 days or more driven only within Moldova. Otherwise a refundable deposit is held and returned in full when the car comes back in the same condition. The minimum age to rent is 18, with a valid driving licence.

How much does airport delivery cost?

Delivery and collection at Chisinau International Airport (KIV) cost €15 and are free on rentals of 7 days or more, at any hour, whatever the car category. Delivery to Iasi Airport (IAS) in Romania costs €175. You can pick the car up in one place and return it in another, for example collect at the airport and drop off in town, at no extra charge within Chisinau.
