Everything you need to know about renting with PlusRent

At PlusRent.md we want the rental process to be as simple and hassle-free as possible. Everything is clear and transparent, with no hidden clauses. If you have any questions, our team is always ready to answer.

About the Guarantee

When picking up the car, a guarantee is paid, which is fully refunded when returning the vehicle, provided that:

1
The car is returned without damage.
2
There are no breaches of contract.
3
No fines, damages or deficiencies are found.

In Case of Accident

Emergency Protocol
Follow these steps immediately
1
Call 112 immediately.
2
Do not leave the accident scene.
3
Urgently inform the PlusRent.md team.
4
If the accident was caused by another participant and the damages are covered by their RCA policy, your guarantee remains untouched, provided you have complied with the contract provisions.

Client Obligations

The client commits to:

  • drive legally and responsibly, without exceeding 120 km/h;
  • not use the vehicle for commercial activities (taxi, driving school, racing, etc.);
  • not leave the territory of the Republic of Moldova without written agreement;
  • not transfer the car to other persons without our permission;
  • return the vehicle clean and in the same condition (except normal wear);
  • take care of the car as if it were their own property.
  • comply with the daily mileage limit.

If you want to take the car outside the country, PlusRent.md can offer you this option based on a personalized offer. Please contact us in advance for details.

Return Process

The handover and return of the vehicle is done based on a report signed by both parties. When returning the vehicle, we will check the mileage. If you have exceeded the included mileage, additional charges of 1.8 lei/km will apply.
