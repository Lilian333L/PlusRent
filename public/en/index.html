ABOUT OUR SERVICE

Car Rental Chisinau: PlusRent

PlusRent is the car rental service in Chisinau and across Moldova with 24/7 delivery to Chisinau International Airport (KIV). Prices from €15/day, third-party liability insurance (RCA) included, no hidden costs. The fleet includes BMW, Mercedes, Audi, Volkswagen, Toyota, all technically inspected and sanitised before every handover.

We offer car rental for a day, a week or long-term with substantial discounts. Delivery to Chisinau Airport €15, free from 7 days at any hour, pickup and return at different locations (Chisinau, Airport, Iasi). Booking takes under 2 minutes.

Round-the-clock customer support at +373 60 000 500: technical assistance, booking changes, route advice. All cars include RCA insurance, air conditioning and GPS. Payment at the end, cash (MDL/EUR) or bank card, no deposit at booking.

Frequently asked questions

Short and direct answers, for any other questions, call us at +373 60 000 500.

What are PlusRent's car rental prices?

Prices start from €15/day for Economy class, €40/day for Business (Audi A4) and €50–€130/day for Premium (Audi A6, Lexus NX 300h, BMW X5, BMW G30, BMW X5 G05). All rates include third-party liability insurance (RCA), 200 km/day mileage and 24/7 support. No deposit at booking, pay when you pick up the car.

Do you deliver cars to Chisinau Airport?

Yes. Delivery to Chisinau International Airport (KIV) costs €15, free from 7 days at any hour. On rentals under 7 days, pickup or return between 18:00 and 08:00 carries a +€15 surcharge per pickup or return. The service is available 24/7.

What documents are required to rent a car?

You need: a valid driving licence and ID card or passport. Drivers aged 35+ rent with no security deposit on rentals of 7 days or more driven only within Moldova; otherwise a refundable security deposit applies. Minimum age is 18, driving experience matters more than age. For foreign citizens we accept international passport and an International Driving Permit if your national licence is not in Latin script. All data is confidential.

What is the minimum rental period?

The minimum rental period is 24 hours. The included mileage allowance is 200 km per day. For bookings of 7+ days or long-term rentals we offer substantial discounts, ask at +373 60 000 500.

What cars are available to rent?

The PlusRent fleet includes: BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, Porsche, Volkswagen, Hyundai, Honda, Toyota. Five categories: Economy (€15+), Crossover (€25+), Business (€40+), Premium (€50+) and Performance (€369+, with a driver only). All cars are technically inspected, sanitised and equipped with air conditioning + GPS.

Are there any hidden fees or extra costs?

No. The price you see is the price you pay. Possible transparent surcharges: Chisinau Airport delivery €15, Iasi Airport +€175, pickup/return outside 08:00–18:00 +€15. Third-party liability insurance (RCA) is included in all rates.

Do you deliver to Iasi Airport (Romania)?

Yes. PlusRent offers delivery to Iasi International Airport (IAS), Romania, for a €175 fee. The Chisinau–Iasi distance is about 3–3.5 hours by road, and our driver assists with the border crossing. One-way rentals are available: pickup in Chisinau, return in Iasi (or vice versa). Convenient for international flights with Wizz Air, Ryanair, TAROM. Call +373 60 000 500 to coordinate the exact time.

How do I book a car?

Book online at plusrent.md in under 2 minutes or call +373 60 000 500. We contact you within 24 hours for confirmation. We accept cash (MDL/EUR), bank card or bank transfer, payment when you pick up the car.

Can I collect the car at Chisinau Airport?
Yes. Delivery to the terminal costs €15 and is free, at any hour, on rentals of 7 days or more. We track the flight number, so a delayed plane costs nothing extra. The details are on the car rental at Chisinau airport page.
What is the difference between car rental and a transfer?
With a rental you drive yourself, from €15 a day. With a transfer our driver takes you, at a fixed price per trip: 45€ for the airport to Chisinau city centre route, with other districts and towns priced separately. If you need a car with a driver for a whole day, the service is a personal driver.
Can I take the rental car outside Moldova?
Yes, to Romania and other EU countries and to Ukraine, but only with our prior approval, decided for each customer individually: in some cases we agree, in others we do not. Trips outside Moldova require a refundable deposit. Tell us your route when you book, on +373 60 000 500.
Do you deliver the car to an address in Chisinau?
Yes, to any address in any district of Chisinau: Botanica, Ciocana, Buiucani, Rascani or the centre. Delivery is free on rentals of 7 days or more; under 7 days it costs €15. On rentals under 7 days, a handover or return between 18:00 and 08:00 costs another €15. You can also collect the car at our office on str. Meșterul Manole 20 or at Chisinau Airport.
How much does it cost to rent a car for a month?
The daily rate drops with the length of the rental: 1-2 days, 3-7, 8-20, 21-45 and 46 days or more. A month is charged at the 21-45 day rate, and from 46 days you get the lowest one, from €15 a day in the Economy category. RCA insurance and 200 km a day are included, and drivers aged 35+ leave no deposit on 7+ day rentals within Moldova. The rates for every car are on the PlusRent fleet page.
